Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

How Kids World Learning handles your family's information. In short: only what's needed to save each child's progress, never ads or tracking, and a grown-up is always in charge.

The short version

Kids World Learning collects only what it needs to save each child's learning progress for their family, and nothing more.

  • A grown-up creates the account with an email address or Sign in with Apple. Children never sign in.
  • For each child, the grown-up enters a first name, age and learning level. We never ask for a last name, photo, voice or location.
  • The app saves lesson progress and stars, so they're there on every iPad the family uses.
  • No ads, no tracking and no chat. We never sell or share anyone's information for marketing.
  • The app is free to download. One optional purchase, the Family unlock, is made through Apple and only in the grown-up area.
  • A grown-up can change or delete a child's profile, or the whole account, at any time in the app.

Who we are and how to reach us

Kids World Learning is made by Welet Pojulu ("we" or "us"). For any question about this policy or your family's information, or to make a request, email weletpojulu@gmail.com. We'll reply as soon as we can, and within 30 days at most.

Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored scrambled (hashed). We never see them.

With Sign in with Apple, Apple shares only an email address with us. You can choose Apple's “Hide My Email”, and then we receive a private relay address instead. We don't ask Apple for your name.

We never collect last names, photos, video, voice recordings, location, contacts, or anything a child types or says. The app doesn't use the camera, microphone or location. Tracing practice in Writing and drawings in the art studio stay on the screen and are never saved or uploaded. The teacher's voice is made on the iPad itself, so no text or audio is sent anywhere to be read aloud. We don't use advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools.

Technical logs. Like most online services, the servers that hold the account may keep short-term technical logs, such as the IP address and time of a request, to keep the service secure and working.

The Family unlock purchase

Kids World Learning is free to download, and some lessons, games and word sets are part of an optional one-time Family unlock. Only a grown-up can buy it, after the grown-up gate. Children see a key and “Ask a grown-up”, never a price or a buy button.

The purchase is made and processed entirely by Apple under your Apple Account. We never see card or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns the unlock, and keeps that yes-or-no answer on the iPad so it works offline. Nothing about the purchase is saved in our database. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How we use information

We use it only to run Kids World Learning for your family:

  • to sign the grown-up in and keep the account secure;
  • to show each child their own name, teacher and lessons at the right level;
  • to save progress and stars, and keep them in sync across the family's iPads;
  • to show the grown-up a progress summary in the grown-up area;
  • to send account emails a grown-up asks for, such as confirming an email address or resetting a password.

We don't use it for advertising or profiling, we don't sell it, and we don't send marketing emails.

Where it's stored and who we share it with

Information is saved in two places:

  • On the iPad, in the app's private storage with iPadOS file protection, so lessons work offline. Signing out removes it from that iPad.
  • In our online database, run by Supabase, so progress follows the family to their other iPads. It is stored on servers in the United States.

We don't sell or rent anyone's information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with the companies that run the app for us, and only for that job:

Children's privacy

Kids World Learning is made for children ages 3 to 10, and a grown-up is always in charge of their information.

  • Only a grown-up can create the account and add a child. Adding or changing a child, opening Settings or Progress, buying the Family unlock, and deleting data all sit behind a grown-up gate: press and hold for 3 seconds, then enter a PIN.
  • The grown-up gives the child's information and consents to its use by creating the child's profile. We collect only what's listed above, and never more than the app needs.
  • Children can't share anything. There's no chat, messaging, posting, photos, purchases, or links to other apps or websites in the children's area.
  • Parents stay in control. A parent or guardian can review a child's information in Progress and Settings, change it, or delete it at any time. Deleting the child's profile stops any further collection. You can also email us to ask what we hold or to have it deleted.

We built the app with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws in other countries in mind. If you believe a child's information reached us without a parent's consent, email us and we'll delete it.

Keeping, changing and deleting information

We keep information only while the account is open, and a grown-up can change or delete it at any time:

  • Change a child's name, age, level, picture or teacher in Settings (grown-up area).
  • Delete a child's profile in Settings ▸ Delete profile. Their profile, stars and progress are removed from the iPad and from our database.
  • Delete the whole account in Progress ▸ Account ▸ Delete account. This removes the grown-up account and every child's profile and progress, and it can't be undone.
  • Signing out removes the family's saved information from that iPad. It stays in the account for the next sign-in.

Deleted information is removed from our live database right away. Our provider's backups may keep it for up to 7 days before it's overwritten.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help within 30 days.

How we protect information

  • Everything between the app and our database travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Database security rules lock each family's information to their own account, so one family can never see another's.
  • On the iPad, saved information uses iPadOS file protection, so it's encrypted while the iPad is locked.
  • Grown-up settings and the Family unlock sit behind a hold-and-PIN gate, so children can't change or buy anything.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell grown-ups in the app or by email. If a change affects how we use children's information, we'll ask for a parent's consent again before it applies.
