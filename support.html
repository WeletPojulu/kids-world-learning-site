Help and support

Last updated November 23, 2026

Answers to common questions about Kids World Learning, the learning app for children from Pre-K to Grade 5 on iPad.

Getting into the grown-up area

Tap Grown-ups, press and hold the circle for 3 seconds until the ring fills, then enter your family's 4-digit PIN. Progress, Settings, Account and the Family unlock are all in there.

Forgot the PIN? Tap Forgot PIN? on the lock screen. After you confirm your account password, or Sign in with Apple, you can choose a new PIN. It changes on every family iPad.

Signing in and passwords

  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in page tap Forgot password?. We email you a 6-digit code; type it with a new password. Each code works once and expires after an hour.
  • No email arrived? Check your junk or spam folder, wait a minute and tap Send again.
  • Same progress on another iPad: sign in there with the same email, or the same Apple Account for Sign in with Apple.

The Family unlock

Pre-K, Kindergarten, the first lesson of every higher level, the art studio and the first word set in each language are free. The one-time Family unlock opens every other lesson, Memory match, Pop the bubbles, Picture puzzle and every word set. There's no subscription.

  • To buy: open the grown-up area and tap the key button, or tap any gold key and choose Get a grown-up.
  • New iPad or reinstalled the app? Go to Grown-ups ▸ Account ▸ Restore purchase.
  • Family Sharing: with Apple Family Sharing turned on, the unlock works for everyone in your family group.
  • “Waiting for approval”: Ask to Buy is on for that Apple Account. The family organizer approves it on their own device.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and voices

  • No sound? Check the iPad's volume and that it isn't on silent, then tap the speaker button on the screen to hear the line again.
  • Spanish, French or Arabic words sound English? The iPad is missing a voice for that language. Open the iPad's Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Spoken Content ▸ Voices, pick the language and download a voice (the Enhanced ones sound best).
  • For the most natural English teacher voice, download a Premium or Enhanced English voice in the same place, then choose it in Grown-ups ▸ Settings ▸ Reading aloud.

Children and accounts

  • Add a child: Grown-ups ▸ Add learner.
  • Change a child's name, level, picture or teacher: Grown-ups ▸ Settings.
  • Delete a child's profile: Grown-ups ▸ Settings ▸ Delete profile.
  • Delete the whole account: Grown-ups ▸ Account ▸ Delete account. This can't be undone.

What you need

An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. An internet connection is needed to sign in and to sync between iPads; lessons keep working offline.

When you write to us, it helps to include the app version, shown in Grown-ups ▸ Account ▸ Help and privacy.

Questions?
